Movement of charged particles

Recall that for parallel plates:
    
    E = V/d 
    
    where V = potential difference
    and d = plate separation


Between the parallel plates:
    - any particle in between the plates experiences the same force with the same
      magnitude and same direction.
    - magnitude + direction are constant.

The particle (if the particle is negatively charged) moves in the opposite direction
to the field lines.

Since:
    F = Eq and F = ma          acceleration is constant

    Eq = ma
    a = (Eq)/m

    Since E = V/d,

    a = (Vq)/(md)

Since the field between parallel plates is uniform (i.e. the magnitude and direction
of the field is the same at all points), the acceleration of a charged particle in
the uniform electric field is constant.
