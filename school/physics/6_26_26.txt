Electric Potential
 
Electric Potential is defined as:

The work done per unit POSITIVE charge to bring a charge from infinity to a point in
an electric field.

Electric Potential is measured in volts.

For a point charge, the formula is:
    1/4π epsilon_0 * Q/r

To find the potential energy, we multiply the potential by the charge
E = Vq

NB: If the potential energy is positive, then that indicates repulsion & energy is
stored.
If the potential energy is negative, then that indicates attraction & energy has been 
released.
